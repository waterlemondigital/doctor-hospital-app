### Overview
Mount Elizabeth Hospital (Orchard) is a private acute-care hospital in Singapore’s Orchard district and part of the Parkway healthcare network. It is known for a broad range of specialist-led medical and surgical services and receives enquiries from overseas patients seeking specialist assessment, planned procedures and second opinions. The hospital works with a large community of specialist doctors practising in and around its medical centre. Clinical expertise and appointment availability should be confirmed directly with the hospital and the individual specialist. Official website: https://www.mountelizabeth.com.sg/ . Official hospital liver-transplant programme material identifies a multidisciplinary team spanning transplant hepatology, gastroenterology, infectious diseases, respiratory medicine, cardiology, anaesthesiology and general surgery: https://www.mountelizabeth.com.sg/docs/default-source/default-document-library/mount-elizabeth-orchard-liver-transplant-programme.pdf

### Services
- Specialist consultations, diagnostic assessment and planned inpatient treatment.
- 24-hour Accident & Emergency / walk-in services.
- Cardiology and cardiovascular assessment, including specialist-led investigation and treatment.
- Cancer assessment and specialist oncology consultations; specific treatment plans depend on the tumour type and treating team.
- Gastroenterology and hepatology, including complex liver disease assessment and liver-transplant services through the hospital's identified programme.
- General and specialist surgery, subject to the relevant specialist and hospital service availability.
- Renal medicine and kidney-disease care.
- Respiratory medicine and pulmonary assessment.
- Diagnostic imaging and laboratory testing as arranged by the treating team.
- Multidisciplinary perioperative and critical-care support for complex treatment pathways.
