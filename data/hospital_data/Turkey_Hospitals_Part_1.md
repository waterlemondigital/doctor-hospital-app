### Overview
Anadolu Medical Center is a private multispecialty hospital located in Gebze, Kocaeli, on the eastern side of the Istanbul metropolitan area. It is particularly known for its oncology services and multidisciplinary cancer-care model, including bone marrow transplantation and organ-specific tumour boards. The hospital states that it has a strategic cooperation with Johns Hopkins Medicine. Its International Services Department supports patients travelling from abroad with treatment and appointment planning, medical guidance, second-opinion requests and communication support. The hospital's international-patient information also describes accommodation for patients and companions at the Anadolu Titanic Comfort Hotel within the hospital premises. The centre treats patients across oncology, cardiovascular care, women's health and IVF, neurological sciences, surgical specialties, internal medicine, diagnostics and imaging. The hospital's published doctor directory and specialty pages identify the doctors listed below; availability and department rosters should be reconfirmed directly before patient referral.

### Services

- Gynecology and obstetrics, including high-risk pregnancy care and selected minimally invasive procedures.
