**Data rules**

- Where a schedule is not published, the file says so and directs the developer to the official appointment route. It does not invent OPD timings.
- Do not infer personal WhatsApp numbers or laboratory connections.
- This is a curated starting set of 30 doctors, not an exhaustive list or an objective ranking of “most trusted” clinicians.
